World Builder

Builder tools

Draw the route

Click grid cells in order. Consecutive points are connected into the route.

Elevation brush

Paint the chosen elevation onto each clicked grid block.

Add scenery

Pine tree — click a block to place

Plants

One (1), Few (5), or Dense (14) per block. Plants on the road are omitted.

Buildings

Paint route tiles to line both sides with road-facing buildings. Tight bends may fit fewer.

Other / erase

Paint ground

Grass — click a block to paint

Ground paint changes the terrain material for the entire 50 × 50 m block without changing its elevation or scenery.

Hill stamp

The clicked block becomes the peak; outer rings blend from their current heights toward it.

Smooth terrain

Click and drag to select a rectangle. Every block in it is smoothed together from the original elevations of its surrounding blocks.
