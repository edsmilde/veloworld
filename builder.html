VELO WORLD

World Builder

1. Draw the route

Click grid cells in order. Consecutive points are connected into the route.

2. Shape the land

Elevation brush paints the chosen value. Hill stamp creates a graduated hill or valley with the chosen peak. Smooth averages terrain around each click. Grid blocks are about 50 m across.

3. Add scenery

Select a cell in Scenery mode to place the chosen item.
